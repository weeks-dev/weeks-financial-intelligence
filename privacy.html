Privacy Policy

Weeks Financial Intelligence · Effective September 2026

Summary

Weeks Financial Intelligence is private, internal-use software operated by Weeks' Family Funeral Homes ("the Company"). It is used only by the Company's own authorized personnel, to view the Company's own financial records. It has no public users and collects no information from anyone outside the Company.

Information accessed

The Application reads financial and accounting data from the Company's own QuickBooks Online account, including:

  • Financial statements and summary reports
  • Transaction records, including dates, amounts, accounts, and descriptions
  • Vendor and customer names as recorded in the Company's accounting system
  • Receivables and payables balances

All such information belongs to the Company. The Application accesses no data belonging to any other organization.

How information is used

Information is used solely to present financial reports to authorized Company management for internal business purposes: financial review, operational management, budgeting, and internal auditing.

How information is stored

The Application does not maintain its own copy of the Company's financial records. Data is retrieved from QuickBooks Online when a report is requested and is not retained afterward.

Credentials used to connect to QuickBooks Online are stored in Microsoft Azure Key Vault, a managed secrets service, and are accessible only to the Application and to Company administrators.

How information is protected

  • All connections to QuickBooks Online use encrypted transport.
  • The Application holds read-only access and cannot alter accounting records.
  • Access is restricted to authorized Company personnel.
  • Credentials are stored in a dedicated managed secrets service, never in application code or configuration files.

Information sharing

The Company does not sell, rent, or share financial information accessed through the Application with any third party. Information is disclosed outside the Company only where required by law, or to the Company's own professional advisors — such as its accountants and auditors — acting under a duty of confidentiality.

Third-party services

The Application relies on Intuit QuickBooks Online as the source of financial data, and on Microsoft Azure and Microsoft 365 for hosting and user access. Each of these providers operates under its own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Because the Application retains no copy of financial data, there is no stored data to delete. Authorization may be revoked at any time by disconnecting the Application from within QuickBooks Online, which immediately ends its access.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above indicates the most recent revision.

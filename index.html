Weeks Financial Agent

Internal financial reporting for Weeks' Family Funeral Homes

About this application

Weeks Financial Agent is private, internal-use software operated by Weeks' Family Funeral Homes. It reads financial data from the company's own QuickBooks Online account and presents it to authorized company management through Microsoft 365.

This application is not offered to the public, is not available for download or purchase, and has no customers other than Weeks' Family Funeral Homes itself.

What it does

  • Retrieves financial reports — profit and loss, balance sheet, receivables and payables aging, vendor spend, and account transaction detail.
  • Presents those reports to authorized company managers for operational review and internal auditing.
  • Operates on a strictly read-only basis.

What it does not do

The application cannot create, modify, delete, or post accounting records. It holds no write permissions of any kind. It does not transmit financial data to any third party, and it does not sell, share, or disclose company information outside the organization.
